# App Review Notes — Trader_7_12 Pro

## Product purpose

Trader_7_12 Pro is a read-only market-information and analytical application.

It does not:
- place orders;
- execute trades;
- hold customer funds;
- manage brokerage accounts;
- provide a payment service;
- provide a cryptocurrency wallet or exchange.

It retrieves market information from configured external data services and presents analytical context.

## Main screens

### Market Radar

The Market Radar scans the supported Russian equity universe and reports:
- market regime;
- benchmark context;
- coverage;
- liquidity;
- daily-quality qualification;
- strict candidate/watch status.

A neutral market regime can legitimately produce zero strict Long/Short candidates.

### Futures Intelligence

The Futures screen shows the locked front-contract universe and combines:
- MOEX futures market information;
- Open Interest;
- current-day turnover;
- authoritative BCS Base Asset mapping;
- intraday Base Asset change;
- real BCS trade-flow information;
- liquidity;
- signal context.

The application intentionally does not create synthetic market data.

## Data availability

If an authoritative source does not provide a required series, the corresponding value remains unavailable. The reviewer may therefore see an incomplete-data status for one or more instruments.

This is intentional product behavior, not an error condition.

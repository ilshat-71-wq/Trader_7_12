### Description

Trader_7_12 Pro is a read-only market intelligence terminal for traders who need a compact view of Russian equity and futures markets.

The application combines two complementary views:

MARKET RADAR
• Scans the supported equity universe.
• Measures market regime and relative strength.
• Applies daily-quality and absolute-liquidity filters.
• Reports coverage and skipped instruments transparently.
• Shows strict Long/Short candidates only when the configured evidence gates are satisfied.

FUTURES INTELLIGENCE
• Tracks the locked front-contract universe.
• Shows Open Interest and OI change.
• Shows current-day monetary turnover.
• Measures current liquidity from real market activity.
• Connects futures to their real Base Asset when authoritative mapping is available.
• Uses real intraday Base Asset change and real money-flow evidence.
• Shows signal, probability, action structure, and entry zone.

DATA QUALITY BY DESIGN

Trader_7_12 Pro does not manufacture missing market data. If an authoritative Base Asset, candle series, or other required evidence is unavailable, the corresponding information remains unavailable rather than being replaced with an estimate.

The application is read-only. It does not place orders, manage brokerage accounts, or execute trades.

Market data availability depends on the configured data provider and current market conditions. The application is designed to show the quality and completeness of the evidence available at scan time.

Trader_7_12 Pro is a market-information and analytical tool. It is not a broker, exchange, investment adviser, or order-execution service.

## App Review notes draft

Trader_7_12 Pro is a read-only market-information application. It does not place orders, connect to a brokerage trading interface, or execute transactions.

The app displays market data obtained from configured external data services. The Futures Intelligence screen combines MOEX futures-market information with authoritative BCS Base Asset mapping and BCS intraday market data.

The app intentionally does not fabricate missing market data. Some instruments can therefore show incomplete evidence when a required source series is unavailable.

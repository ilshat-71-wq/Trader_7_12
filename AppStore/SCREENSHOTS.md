## Rules

- Screenshots must show the actual app.
- Do not fabricate market data.
- Avoid screenshots dominated by empty diagnostics.
- Do not use the splash screen as the primary screenshot.
- Keep overlays minimal.
- Do not include private BCS credentials or personal information.
